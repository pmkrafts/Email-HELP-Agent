# Charges, duplicate charges and invoice questions

- Card statements show charges as "AURORA*ELEC".
- A duplicate charge is usually an authorisation hold and auto-reverses within
  3 business days; if it persists, the billing team issues a reversal.
- Written confirmations for banks: the billing team can provide one within 2 business days.
- Invoice corrections (wrong price or VAT) are handled by the billing team, usually within
  2 business days.
