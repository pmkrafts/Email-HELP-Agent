# Refund & Returns Policy

- Return window: 30 days from delivery, unused items in original packaging.
- Damaged on arrival: customer chooses replacement or full refund; photo evidence required.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the
  returned item is inspected by the warehouse.
- Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.
- Support agents must never promise an instant or same-day refund: all refunds require a
  case note and approval by the billing team.
- Duplicate charges are verified by the billing team; confirmed duplicates are reversed
  within 3 business days.
