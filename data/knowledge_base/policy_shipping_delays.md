# Shipping & Delay Compensation Policy

- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days. Express: 1-2 business days.
- If delivery is more than 5 business days past the promised date: shipping fee is
  refunded and a 10% goodwill credit is offered (requires support lead approval).
- If more than 10 business days late, the customer may choose full cancellation with refund.
- Agents must not promise a new delivery date unless tracking confirms the parcel has
  shipped; otherwise state that the warehouse is investigating and give a follow-up date.
